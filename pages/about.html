Our Story

Built for the Nigerian
classroom.

NaijaLearn was born from a simple frustration — quality lesson materials existed, but were scattered, unstructured, and hard to find. We decided to fix that.

Our Mission

Every Nigerian student deserves a prepared teacher.

We know what it's like — teachers spending hours searching WhatsApp groups for a lesson note, parents printing resources from shady websites, students going into exams underprepared. NaijaLearn exists to end that.

"We believe the future of Nigerian education starts with giving educators the right tools — structured, verified, and accessible to everyone, everywhere in the country."

Our platform lets teachers upload and share lesson notes, past questions, and schemes of work, organised by subject, class, term, and week — so anyone can find exactly what they need in seconds.

What we stand for

Our values.

🎯

Structure over chaos

Every resource is tagged by subject, class, term, and week — so finding the right note takes seconds, not hours.

✅

Quality first

Our verification system ensures the resources on NaijaLearn are accurate, curriculum-aligned, and worth your time.

🤝

Community-driven

Teachers upload, review, and improve each other's work. The platform gets better every time someone contributes.

🌍

Access for all

Free to browse, free to download. NaijaLearn is for every student and teacher, regardless of school type or location.

🇳🇬

Built for Nigeria

Designed around the NERDC curriculum, Nigerian school terms, and the realities of teaching and learning in Nigeria — not adapted from elsewhere.

🔒

Trust and safety

We verify contributors, moderate content, and keep your data private. A platform teachers recommend must be one they can trust completely.
